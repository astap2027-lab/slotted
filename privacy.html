How Slotted uses your data

Last updated 10 November 2026.

The short version. Slotted is a gym planner for students who play sport. Without an account, everything stays on your device. With an account, your training is stored so it syncs between your devices. We never sell your data, never show ads, and never use it for marketing.

Who runs Slotted

Slotted is made and run by Axel, a student in Sydney, Australia. If you have a question about your data or want to ask for a copy or for it to be deleted, email slottedgym@gmail.com.

Without an account

Everything you enter (your timetable, routine, logged sets, check-ins and coach chats) is saved in your browser on your device. It isn’t sent to us. Clearing your browser’s data for this site deletes it, so download a backup now and then (Settings, then Download backup).

If you sign in with Google

  • Google tells us your name, your email address and an ID for your Google account. We never see your Google password, and we don’t get access to anything else in your Google account.
  • Your Slotted data is stored on Cloudflare’s servers so it can sync between your devices. That includes your timetable, routine, workouts and logged sets, check-ins, settings and coach chats.
  • We use one cookie to keep you signed in. It lasts 60 days, or until you sign out. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and there are no analytics.

Health-related information

Some of what you record is about your health: daily check-ins (sleep, soreness, energy and, if you add them, hours slept, tiredness and stress), how hard each exercise felt, and the muscle recovery estimates Slotted works out from your training. We treat this as sensitive. It’s used only to plan and adjust your training and to answer your questions, it’s only visible to you, and it’s never sold or shared for advertising.

Recovery and fatigue figures are estimates from what you’ve recorded. They aren’t medical measurements, and Slotted doesn’t give medical advice. If something hurts, see a physio or GP.

The AI coach

  • When you ask the coach a question, your question and a summary of your training are sent to Anthropic’s Claude API to write the answer. The summary includes your goal and plan, this week’s sessions and commitments, today’s check-in, recovery estimates and recent sets. It doesn’t include your name or email address.
  • If you type a change to your week (for example “I finish school at 1:05 on Tuesdays”) and Slotted can’t work it out on its own, that sentence and your commitments for the week are sent to the same service to read it. Nothing changes until you approve it.
  • If you add a photo of your timetable, the photo is shrunk on your device and sent to the same service to read the days and times. We don’t store the photo. Timetables often show your name and school, so crop those out first if you’d rather not share them.
  • Anthropic processes this to provide the answer, under its own privacy policy and commercial terms, and doesn’t use it to train its models.
  • Your Strava data is never sent to the coach.

Strava

  • If you connect Strava, we store an encrypted access key that lets Slotted read your activities.
  • Your activities are fetched when you open your week and shown only to you. We don’t store them, share them, or send them to the AI coach.
  • You can disconnect Strava at any time in Settings, which also removes Slotted’s access on Strava’s side.

Where your data is kept, and for how long

  • Slotted runs on Cloudflare. Account data is kept in a Cloudflare database until you delete your account. Cloudflare, Google and Anthropic may handle data on servers outside Australia.
  • To stop the coach being overused, we count questions per internet address (IP address) each day. That count is deleted after 48 hours.
  • Data is sent over encrypted connections. Only a scrambled version of your sign-in token is stored, so a leaked database couldn’t be used to sign in as you.

Age

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. If you’re under 18, check with a parent or guardian before you create an account. Slotted works fully without an account, with everything kept on your device. If you’re a parent and think your child has created an account they shouldn’t have, email us and we’ll delete it.

Your choices

  • Get a copy: Settings, then Download backup saves everything as a file.
  • Delete your account: Settings, then Delete account removes your account, your synced data and your Strava connection from our servers straight away.
  • Delete data on this device: Settings, then Delete everything on this device.
  • Fix or ask about your data: email slottedgym@gmail.com and we’ll reply as soon as we can.

Changes to this page
