How Slotted uses your data

Last updated [date]. Questions: [your email address].

Without an account

Everything you enter (your week, routine, logged sets and check-ins) is saved in your browser on your device. It isn’t sent to us.

With an account

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a Google account ID. We don’t see your Google password.
  • Your Slotted data is stored on Cloudflare’s servers so it can sync between your devices.
  • We use a cookie to keep you signed in. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Strava

  • If you connect Strava, we store an encrypted access key that lets Slotted read your activities.
  • Your activities are fetched when you open your week and shown only to you. We don’t store them, share them, or send them to the AI coach.
  • You can disconnect Strava at any time in Settings, which also removes Slotted’s access on Strava’s side.

The AI coach

When you ask the coach a question, your question and a short summary of your plan, this week, today’s check-in and recent lifts are sent to Anthropic’s Claude API to write the answer. Anthropic processes this under its own privacy policy. The coach gives general training guidance, not medical advice.

Deleting your data

Settings, then Delete account removes your account, your synced data and your Strava connection from our servers. Data saved in your browser stays on your device until you clear it, or use Settings, then Delete everything.
